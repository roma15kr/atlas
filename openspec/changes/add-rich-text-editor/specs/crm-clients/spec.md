## ADDED Requirements

### Requirement: Formatted client notes and comments
Client notes (up to 10,000 characters) and client comments (1–5,000 characters) SHALL be formatted text (see `rich-text`), written in the editor and shown formatted on the client card. Whoever may edit the client SHALL be able to tick checklist items in its notes in place. Comment checklists SHALL be read-only.

#### Scenario: Formatted comment
- **WHEN** a manager adds a comment with a table of prices
- **THEN** everyone who can see the client sees the table in the comment feed
