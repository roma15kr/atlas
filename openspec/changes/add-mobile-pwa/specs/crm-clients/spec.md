## ADDED Requirements

### Requirement: Client card on a phone
At or below 640px the client card SHALL show «Позвонить» and «Написать» as large buttons at the top when the client has a phone or an email, opening the phone's dialler or mail app, and SHALL stack its sections one under another.

#### Scenario: Calling a dealer
- **WHEN** a manager on a phone opens a client with a phone number and taps «Позвонить»
- **THEN** the phone's dialler opens with that number
