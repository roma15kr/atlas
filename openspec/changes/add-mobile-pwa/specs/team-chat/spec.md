## ADDED Requirements

### Requirement: Chat on a phone
At or below 640px the chat screen SHALL show either the conversation list or one conversation with a «К списку» back button, and the message box SHALL stay at the bottom of the screen.

#### Scenario: Opening a conversation
- **WHEN** a user on a phone taps a conversation in the list
- **THEN** the list is replaced by the conversation, and «К списку» returns to the list
