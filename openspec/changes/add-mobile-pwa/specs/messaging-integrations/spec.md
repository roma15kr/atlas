## ADDED Requirements

### Requirement: Telegram inbox on a phone
At or below 640px the Telegram inbox SHALL show either the customer list or one conversation with a «Назад» button, and the reply box SHALL stay at the bottom of the screen.

#### Scenario: Answering a customer on the road
- **WHEN** a manager on a phone opens a customer from the list
- **THEN** the conversation fills the screen with the reply box at the bottom, and «Назад» returns to the list
